/// WACC - Weighted Average Cost of Capital
WACC = (E/V) × Re + (D/V) × Rd × (1 − Tax) where E = equity, D = debt, V = E+D, Re = cost of equity (CAPM), Rd = cost of debt. WACC is the minimum return required to create value. NPV > 0 at WACC means the project generates value above the cost of capital.

/// key economic formulas
NPV: NPV = Σ [CFt / (1+r)^t] for t = 0 to n [USD]
IRR: Solve NPV = 0 → Σ [CFt / (1+IRR)^t] = 0 (iterative)
Payback period: Year when cumulative undiscounted cash flow turns positive
Discounted payback: Year when cumulative discounted cash flow turns positive
Profitability Index PI: PI = (NPV + CAPEX) / CAPEX = 1 + NPV/CAPEX
WACC: WACC = (E/V)×Re + (D/V)×Rd×(1−Tax)
CAPM cost of equity Re: Re = Rf + β×ERP + CRP
After-tax cost of debt: Rd_AT = Rd × (1 − Tax)
Arps decline (hyperbolic): q(t) = qi / (1 + b×Di×t)^(1/b)
Arps decline (exponential b=0): q(t) = qi × exp(−Di×t)
EUR (exponential): EUR = qi / Di × (1 − exp(−Di×t_aban))
EUR (hyperbolic): EUR = (qi^b / ((1−b)×Di)) × (qi^(1−b) − qmin^(1−b))
Economic limit: q_EL = OPEX / (Price × (1 − Royalty − Tax))
Revenue CF: CF = Q×Price×(1−Royalty) − OPEX − CapexMaintenance − Taxes
Govt take: GovernmentTake = (Royalty + TaxOnProfit) × Gross Revenue
EMV (Expected Monetary Value): EMV = Psucc×NPV_success − Pfail×|NPV_failure|
Sensitivity: ΔNPV/ΔPrice = Σ [Q_t×(1−Royalty)×(1−Tax) / (1+r)^t]
