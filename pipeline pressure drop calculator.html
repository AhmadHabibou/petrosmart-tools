/// gas pipeline - Weymouth and Panhandle equations
Gas pipeline formulas:
Weymouth (short, high-rate lines): f = C/D^(1/3), good for small-diameter pipelines.
Panhandle A (intermediate efficiency): E factor 0.92 typical. Good for medium-pressure gas.
Panhandle B (modified, high-pressure): More accurate at high Reynolds numbers.
General Darcy-Weisbach with Colebrook-White friction factor is most accurate.

/// key formulas - pipeline pressure drop
Darcy-Weisbach: ΔP = f × (L/D) × (ρ × v²/2) [Pa]
Total pressure drop: ΔP_total = ΔP_friction + ΔP_elevation + ΔP_acceleration
ΔP_elevation: ρ × g × Δz [Pa] (+ for uphill)
Reynolds number: Re = ρ × v × D / μ [dimensionless]
Friction factor (Colebrook-White): 1/√f = -2 × log10(ε/(3.7D) + 2.51/(Re×√f))
Swamee-Jain (explicit): f = 0.25 / [log10(ε/(3.7D) + 5.74/Re^0.9)]²
Weymouth (gas): Q = 3.7435×10⁻³ × D^(8/3) × √((P1²-P2²)/(γg×T×Z×L)) [Sm3/d]
Panhandle A: Q = 435.87 × E × (T_sc/P_sc)^1.0788 × (P1²-P2²)^0.5394 / (γg^0.4606×T^0.5394×L^0.5394×D^1.0788)
Hazen-Williams (water): Q = 0.2785 × C_HW × D^2.63 × S^0.54 [m3/s, S = slope = ΔP/(ρgL)]
Beggs-Brill Froude: Fr = vm/√(g×D) | NFr = vm²/(g×D)
Lockhart-Martinelli X: X_LM = (ΔP/L)_liquid/(ΔP/L)_gas)^0.5
Erosion velocity (API RP 14E): Ve = C/√ρ_mix [ft/s], C=100-200
Slug frequency: fs ≈ 0.025 × (vsL + vsg)² / D [Hz] (approximate Taitel-Dukler)
