/// key formulas - drilling hydraulics (API RP 13D)
Flow velocity in pipe (m/s): v = Q(bbl/min) × 0.3208 / D_ID(inch)²
Flow velocity in annulus (m/s): v_ann = Q × 0.3208 / (Dh² - Dp²)
Reynolds number (Bingham Plastic - pipe):
Re_BP = 109000 × ρ × v × D / PV [ρ in g/cm3, v in m/s, D in inch, PV in cP]
Laminar/Turbulent threshold (Bingham): Re_c = 3470 - 1370×(YP/τw)
Fann viscometer - Bingham: PV = R600 - R300 [cP] | YP = R300 - PV [lb/100ft2]
ΔP laminar (Bingham, pipe) [psi]:
ΔP = PV × v × L / (144 × D²) + YP × L / (144 × D)
[PV in cP, v in ft/s, L in ft, D in inch, YP in lb/100ft2]
ΔP laminar (Bingham, annulus) [psi]:
ΔP_ann = PV × v_ann × L / (144 × (Dh-Dp)²) + YP × L / (144 × (Dh-Dp))
ECD [g/cm3]: ECD = MW + APL_psi / (0.052 × TVD_ft)
ECD [g/cm3] = MW + (APL_bar × 1000) / (0.0981 × TVD_m)
Bit nozzle velocity [ft/s]: v_n = Q × 3117 / (Cd × TFA_in2)
[Cd = 0.95 discharge coefficient, TFA in inch2]
Bit pressure drop [psi]: ΔP_bit = ρ × v_n² / (1120) [ρ in ppg, v_n in ft/s]
HSI [HP/inch2]: HSI = Q × ΔP_bit / (1714 × A_bit_in2)
JIF [lbf]: JIF = 0.01823 × Cd × ρ_ppg × Q² / TFA
Optimal bit ΔP (BHPP criterion): ΔP_bit_opt = 0.65 × ΔP_standpipe
Equivalent ΔP for cuttings: APL_min = (annular velocity min) × ρ × D_h
Swab/surge pressure: ΔP_swab = Cs × 0.052 × TVD | ΔP_surge = Cg × 0.052 × TVD
