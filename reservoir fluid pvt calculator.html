/// key PVT correlations - formulas
Standing (1947) - Bo [RB/STB]:
Bo = 0.9759 + 0.000120 × [Rs × (γg/γo)^0.5 + 1.25×T_F]^1.2
where γo = 141.5/(API+131.5), T_F = temperature in °F

Standing (1947) - Rs [scf/STB]:
Rs = γg × (P/18.2 + 1.4)^1.205 × 10^(0.0125×API - 0.00091×T_F)

Vasquez-Beggs (1980) - Rs:
Rs = C1 × γgs × P^C2 × 10^(C3×API/T_R)
C1,C2,C3 depend on API (above/below 30°API)

Al-Marhoun (1988) - Bo:
Bo = -1.001621 + (0.000005 × a) where a = Rs^0.742390 × γg^0.323294 × γo^(-1.202040) × T_R^0.0

Undersaturated oil compressibility co [psi-1]:
co = (Rs + 17.2×T_F - 1180×γg + 12.61×API - 1433) / (10^5 × P)
or Vasquez-Beggs: co = 1433 + 5Rs + 17.2×T_F - 1180×γg + 12.61×API) / (10^5 × P)

Dead oil viscosity (Beal, 1946):
μod = (0.32 + 1.8×10^7/API^4.53) × (360/(T_F+200))^a, a = 10^(0.43+8.33/API)

Live oil viscosity (Chew-Connally, 1959):
μo = A × μod^B, A and B functions of Rs

Gas Z-factor (Hall-Yarborough):
Ppr = P/Ppc, Tpr = T/Tpc | Solve: -APpr + [y+y²+y³-y⁴]/(1-y)³ - (14.76/Tpr - 9.76/Tpr² + 4.58/Tpr³)y + (90.7/Tpr - 242.2/Tpr² + 42.4/Tpr³)y^(2.18+2.82/Tpr) = 0
Z = APpr / y

Gas formation volume factor Bg [res bbl/scf]:
Bg = 0.00504 × Z × T_R / P [T in °R = (T_F+460), P in psia]

Gas viscosity (Lee-Kesler, 1966):
μg = 10^-4 × K × exp(X × (ρg/62.4)^Y) where K = (9.4 + 0.02×MW)×T_R^1.5 / (209+19×MW+T_R)
X = 3.5 + 986/T_R + 0.01×MW | Y = 2.4 - 0.2×X
